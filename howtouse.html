FAQ ❓

Which sites are supported?

YouTube (including Shorts), Vimeo and Dailymotion links.

Is it free?

Yes, completely free with no sign-up needed.

Why is my image smaller than expected?

Some videos don't have an HD thumbnail. In that case we fall back to the best size available.

Private videos?

Private or deleted videos can't be fetched. Only public thumbnails are available.

Is it okay to download thumbnails?

Use thumbnails respectfully. Don't reuse them in ways that infringe on the creator's rights or the platform's terms.
